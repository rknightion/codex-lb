# Design
Use explicit hard-owner intent only on a proven singleton pool. Preserve all persisted account exclusions, hard cooldowns, admission limits and newer health generations. Retire stale injected anchor carriers without inventing upstream evidence; retain atomic recovery and quarantine fences. Full context replay is preferred; existing operator-accepted portable fresh-start policy remains available. Explicit anchors, files and ambiguous execution are not permission to switch owners or replay.

Regression evidence must cross selection/route boundaries, and actual pi through the proxy against a fake upstream must validate bounded recovery and tool/lease safety. Separate component checks are insufficient for combined proof.
